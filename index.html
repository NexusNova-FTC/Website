Nexus Nova

Nexus Nova is a student-led rookie FIRST Tech Challenge team based in Waukee, Iowa. Our team is made up of 8 students and 2 coaches, working together to design, build, program, and compete with our robot during the 2026–2027 BIOBUZZ season.

As a new team, we are excited to take on the challenges of FTC while developing our skills in engineering, programming, design, and teamwork. We believe robotics is about more than just competition—it’s about learning, creating, and inspiring others.

Ways You Can Help

  • Financial Donation: Our team is dependent on funding from grants and sponsors. Sponsorships ensure our sustainability and the ability to purchase parts necessary for us to be successful.
  • Fuel the Team: During build season, we spend countless hours in the workshop. Donations of team meals and snacks are always welcome and appreciated. Check out our social media for posts and stories about our future donors.
  • Material Donation: The materials required for building robots are expensive. Therefore, any donation of tools or resources is very much appreciated. For questions regarding specific donations, please email the team.
  • Mentoring: Mentors are vital to team operations. They provide technical and non-technical support while supervising members in the workshop.
